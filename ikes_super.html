How to read it. Each sandwich is filed under its headline protein (the first meat listed). The locations badge shows how widely it's carried: core = on 40+ menus (chain-wide staples), everything else is regional or a single-store exclusive. Every sandwich comes hot on Dutch Crunch with dirty sauce, lettuce & tomato by default, and most meat builds can be made vegan on request. Multi-meat combos (e.g. Matt Cain) sit under their first-listed meat.
Source: Ike's online ordering menus (oxb.pxsweb.com / orderexperience.net) across all 116 active locations, pulled 2026-07-23. 768 unique item names after dedup; naming and availability vary by store, and in-store-only sandwiches beyond the app aren't captured here.
