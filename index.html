How this was made

Ike's online ordering menus (oxb.pxsweb.com / orderexperience.net) were pulled one location at a time and merged, deduplicated by sandwich name. Protein grouping is derived from each item's first-listed ingredient, so multi-meat combos sit under their lead protein.

Every Ike's sandwich comes hot on Dutch Crunch with dirty sauce, lettuce & tomato by default, and most meat builds can be made vegan on request. Sandwiches marked with the 📍 gold badge are location exclusives — they appear on exactly one store's menu.
